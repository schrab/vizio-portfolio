## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
  <summary>I don't have a precise idea or brief. Can you help?</summary>
  <p>Of course. Most cool projects start with the words "I want something unusual to match the vibe." I will develop a visual concept that will solve your business tasks myself.</p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary>Do you paint only indoors or outdoors as well?</summary>
  <p>I work with any surfaces and scales. From a cozy wall in a coffee shop to a blank facade of a multi-story building. Materials are selected for the task: facade paints do not fade and are climate-resistant.</p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary>How long does the painting take?</summary>
  <p>It all depends on the area of the wall and the detail of the drawing. On average, an interior wall takes 2 to 5 days. More massive facade projects or the creation of complex carved panels are discussed individually. I fix exact deadlines before starting.</p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary>Will it smell bad? Will we have to close the venue?</summary>
  <p>No. For interiors, I use modern water-based acrylic materials or special aerosols with minimal odor that quickly dissipates. If necessary, I can work outside the venue's opening hours.</p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary>What about the custom plywood panels?</summary>
  <p>This is my unique service. I create multi-layered, carved 3D paintings from wood and plywood. They assemble like a constructor and are easily mounted on the wall. This is a premium solution if you want to update the interior but are not ready for full painting work in the room.</p>
</details>
